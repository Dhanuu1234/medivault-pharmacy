About MediVault

MediVault is a full-stack pharmacy management system built with Django. It covers the two sides of a real pharmacy: a storefront where customers can search medicines, upload prescriptions and track orders, and a staff dashboard for managing stock, expiry dates and order fulfilment.

How ordering works

  1. Search or browse the catalogue by category.
  2. Add medicines to your cart — prescription-only items are clearly labelled.
  3. Checkout with your delivery details and, if needed, upload a prescription photo.
  4. Track your order status from "Pending" through to "Delivered" on the Orders page.

Built for pharmacy operations

The staff dashboard tracks low-stock and near-expiry medicines automatically, so restocking decisions happen before a shelf runs empty rather than after.

Tech stack

  • Django 5
  • PostgreSQL / SQLite
  • Whitenoise
  • Gunicorn
  • Render

This is a portfolio / demonstration project. Medicine details shown are general product information and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.
